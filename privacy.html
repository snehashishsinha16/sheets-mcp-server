Privacy Policy

My Sheets Assistant is a personal-use application built by an individual developer to connect their own Google Sheets and Google Drive account to an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), for personal automation of spreadsheet tasks.

This app is not intended for use by the general public. Only the developer's own Google account uses it.

Data access

This app requests access to Google Sheets and Google Drive (metadata only) belonging solely to the developer's own account, to read and write spreadsheet data at the developer's request.

Data storage

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is used only to fulfill the developer's own requests within the app.
